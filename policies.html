Rules & trust

Policies

Delivery, returns, privacy and terms — written in plain language, so you know exactly what to expect.

Delivery

  • Where we deliver. Kampala and surrounding areas — Kampala, Wakiso, Entebbe and Mukono (greater Kampala).
  • How it works. You order on WhatsApp. We confirm your total, including the delivery fee, and agree a delivery day and place with you before anything is dispatched.
  • Delivery fee. The fee depends on your area and the size of your order. It is always agreed on WhatsApp before you pay — never added afterwards.
  • Business customers. Salons and spas receive scheduled deliveries on an agreed day, with an invoice for bank transfer.
  • Outside greater Kampala. Ask us on WhatsApp — we handle it case by case and will tell you the cost before you commit.
  • Delays. If we are late, we message you first. If you no longer want the order after a delay caused by us, you cancel at no cost.

Delivery areas and fees confirmed on WhatsApp · Mon–Sat, 8am – 7pm

Returns and refunds

  • Changed your mind? Unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Message us on WhatsApp with your order code first.
  • Damaged or wrong item? Tell us within 24 hours of delivery with your order code and a photo of what arrived. We replace it or refund you, including the delivery fee.
  • Opened skincare cannot be returned, for hygiene and safety reasons — this is standard for cosmetics.
  • Refund method. Refunds go back through the way you paid: mobile money to the same number, bank transfer to the same account, or cash for cash-on- delivery orders.
  • Refund time. Normally within 3 working days of us confirming the refund in our own records.
  • How we confirm payments. We check our own mobile money, bank or provider records. A screenshot alone is never accepted as proof — see the How to Pay page.

Privacy notice

In line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

  • This website collects nothing. There are no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no tracking tools on these pages. Nothing you do here is stored by the site.
  • What we would collect, and why. When you order on WhatsApp we would receive your name, your phone number, your delivery area or address, and the items you want — only so we can answer you, deliver your order and keep the record of the sale.
  • Who can see it. Only the owners of Smooth Body Works. We do not sell your information, do not share it with advertisers, and do not put it on this website.
  • How long we keep it. Only as long as needed to deliver, answer questions and keep the records required of a business — then it is deleted. Order records are kept only for the period the law requires for business and tax records.
  • WhatsApp's role. Ordering happens inside WhatsApp, so WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to that chat.
  • Your rights. Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office established under that Act.
  • Children. Our products are sold to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp — we respond within 7 days.

  • By messaging us to order, you ask us to supply the items shown on this site; a contract forms when we confirm your order and total on WhatsApp.
  • All prices are in Uganda shillings (UGX) and can change — the price we confirm to you on WhatsApp is the price you pay.
  • Item codes (SBW-01, SBW-02, …) identify products; please quote them so we pack the right thing.
  • Our products are cosmetics for external use only. They are not medicines and do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Patch-test first, keep away from eyes, and see a doctor for persistent skin problems.
