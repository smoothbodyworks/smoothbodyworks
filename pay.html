How to Pay and Order

Ordering happens on WhatsApp. Paying happens with the method that suits you. This page shows every step, every detail, and exactly how we confirm a payment.

This is a student project, payments are not active.

This website is coursework for BUC3106 at Makerere University Business School. No real money moves through it. All account numbers below are obvious SAMPLE numbers, shown only to demonstrate how the payment page would work.

Ordering in three steps

  1. Pick your products On the Products page, note the item code (for example SBW-05) and the price in shillings.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp Tap any “Order on WhatsApp” button and send your item codes, name and delivery area. We reply with the total, including delivery, before you pay.
  3. Pay with your favourite method Choose one of the five options below, pay, and send us the transaction ID. We check our own records and confirm, then we pack and deliver.

How we confirm a payment

  1. We open our own mobile money account, bank statement or provider dashboard and look for your transaction.
  2. We match the amount, the sender details and your item code or reference.
  3. Only then do we mark your order as paid and dispatch it.

A screenshot alone is never accepted as proof of payment. Anyone can edit a picture. We always check our own records first — if our records and your claim do not match, we ask the provider to trace the transaction before anything is sent.

Refunds

If you pay and we cannot deliver your order, we refund you in full through the same method you paid with, after we have checked our records — normally within 3 working days of confirming the mistake. If a product arrives damaged or wrong, tell us on WhatsApp within 24 hours with your order code and we replace it or refund you. For hygiene reasons, opened skincare cannot be returned; unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Delivery fees are refunded only when the error is ours.

Why we chose these payment methods

Our customers live in Uganda, where mobile money is the everyday way to pay, so MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money come first — they work on any phone, cost the customer almost nothing, and settle in seconds. Cash on delivery matters because many first-time buyers want to see and touch the product before money leaves their hand. Bank transfer exists for our business customers — salons and spas — who need an invoice and a paper trail for their accounts. Card payment is offered through a licensed provider so that customers who prefer cards, including people outside Uganda, can pay without us ever touching their card details.

Do we accept cryptocurrency? No. The Bank of Uganda has stated that crypto-currencies such as Bitcoin are not legal tender in Uganda and are not regulated as money in the country, so we do not accept them as payment. Mobile money, bank transfer, cash and cards remain our methods.
