Our story

About Smooth Body Works

A Kampala skincare brand that believes the label should tell you everything.

Who we are

Smooth Body Works makes skincare in Kampala, Uganda. The range grew around one simple rule: print the whole truth on the label. Every jar and bottle carries its full ingredient list, its batch number, and its manufacturing and expiry dates.

The range covers the full face routine — the Smooth & Flourish brightening face cream, the tea tree + salicylic foaming cleanser with a built-in brush, and a family of serums for acne, dark spots, oil balance, hydration and brightening. Each product says on the front what it does and who it is for.

Customers order directly through WhatsApp, where the whole catalogue lives, and our community — the Clear Skin Tribe — follows along on TikTok for tips, offers and real routines.

Made in Kampala, Uganda · Ordered on WhatsApp · Delivered in Kampala and surrounding areas

What we stand for

Our values

Four promises you can check yourself, right on the product.

Honest labels

Full ingredient lists, batch numbers and dates on every product. If it is inside the bottle, it is on the label.

Gentle formulations

The foaming cleanser is soap-free, paraben-free and pH-balanced for acne-prone skin, and formulas are made not to strip or sting.

No bleaching agents

Our products contain no bleaching agents and are made to be safe for all skin types — we brighten and even tone, we do not bleach.

Made in Kampala

Produced locally in Kampala, Uganda, so products reach you fresh and the money stays in the local economy.

What we help with

Skin concerns we are built for

The concerns listed on our own flyer — no miracle claims, just what the formulations are designed to support.

1

Acne & breakouts

Face acne, chest acne, back acne, pimples and blackheads, with salicylic acid and tea tree at the core of the routine.

2

Dark spots & uneven tone

Hyperpigmentation, inner-thigh dark marks and uneven skin tone, supported by tranexamic acid, alpha-arbutin and vitamin C.

3

Texture & hydration

Oily skin, dry skin, fine lines and razor bumps — balanced with niacinamide, hyaluronic acid and mandelic acid.

An honest note

Our products are cosmetics, not medicines. They support your skin, they do not treat medical conditions — if you have a persistent skin problem, please see a doctor. We do not publish customer reviews or testimonials on this site, because this is a student project and we only show claims we can prove from our own labels.
